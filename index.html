Hire Me boundary

What the $150 tier is — and what it is not.

Hire Me is one consulting hour’s worth of access each month: a closed project space, one 60-minute call, and weekday async answers within 48 hours. Entry members get the Academy — feed, lessons, lives, Start here, topic requests, and help with small problems. They do not get private project work with Pascal.

Community homework threads are part of Entry. Hire Me is for private project work, not a fast lane on weekly homework.

In practice, each month

  • One 60-minute project call. Unused calls do not roll over unless both sides agree in writing in the closed area.
  • Async replies within 48 hours on workdays. Weekends and public holidays start the clock on the next workday.
  • Up to eight substantive questions or threads in the closed area. A back-and-forth on the same topic counts as one.
  • If a month is unusually heavy, Pascal may pause new async threads until the next call and will say so within the 48-hour window.
  • Cancel anytime in Skool billing. Closed-area access ends when the paid period ends. Calls already booked in that period are honored if they still fall inside the paid window.

In scope

  • Choosing and shaping a workflow or bot
  • Reviewing your setup and unblocking errors
  • Recommending stack choices
  • Pairing on one concrete automation
  • Writing or editing prompts and skills for your case

Out of scope

  • Unlimited custom software delivery
  • 24/7 support or guaranteed same-day replies
  • Doing your client work end-to-end
  • Legal or tax advice
  • Hosting paid infrastructure on Pascal’s accounts without a separate agreement

After you join Hire Me, the monthly call is booked together in the closed area. A public booking link is not on this page yet.

FAQ

Short answers.

Is there a free trial?

No. There is no free trial and no free sample of the lessons. This page and the example outcomes show what you get.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Membership is monthly, and you can cancel in Skool billing at any time. The $10 entry price stays locked while you stay subscribed. If you cancel and rejoin later, the then-current price applies. Hire Me access ends when the paid period ends.

What do the Skool cards Standard and Premium mean?

Standard is the $10 Entry: feed, lessons, lives, the Start here path, topic requests, and help with small problems. Premium is Hire Me at $150/month: book Pascal 1:1, one 60-minute call a month, weekday async within 48 hours, and project guidance — not unlimited builds.

What do I do first?

Join for $10/month, answer the three questions, open the Start here course, do the first action on your path, and introduce yourself under the pinned post. Late is fine. Catch-up starts at Lesson 1.

What if I only want community help, not a private project?

Stay on Entry. Ask in the feed, follow your path, and request a topic if you are stuck. Hire Me is only for private project work with Pascal.

How do I join or upgrade?

Everything is handled in the Skool community, in USD. This website does not collect payments or account details.
