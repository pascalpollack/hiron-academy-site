Who this is for

People who understand, build, and stay.

For

People who want to understand systems and build them themselves. People who think long term, stay, and support each other. People who, after something fails, ask what their own part was, not just the other person’s. Pascal builds members up to eye level, so later you can tackle bigger things together and pool skills from different fields.

Not for

Silent lurkers. People chasing quick money with AI. Anyone who just watched a creator video and now wants to go self-employed. People who call every ex or ex-friend a narcissist and never look at their own part. Show-offs who think they’re the greatest.

What you get here

A place where you can be yourself.

No masks, no pretending.

  • Nobody judges how you write: AI or no AI, upper or lower case, typos and swapped letters, all caps or none.
  • Nobody judges how you reply: right away or days later, with a greeting or without, with names or without.
  • Dyslexia, autism, ADHD or anything else that doesn’t fit the ‘norm’ doesn’t matter here. Some of the sharpest minds think differently.
  • What you’re into in your private life is your business. Nobody judges it.
  • Only the content counts. Content is never dismissed because of how it looks.
  • Arguments, constructive criticism and real discussion. No shaming, no moralizing, no being put in your place.
  • Unemployed or running a company, nobody cares. Everyone is treated as an equal.
  • The only thing that counts is what you can do. What you can’t do yet is never used against you. You get help to get better at it.
  • Your potential gets seen and pushed. And if it turns out what you came for isn’t what you really want, we’ll talk about it: is this truly you, or something you think you’re supposed to be good at?
  • Need advice on something personal, not tech, like a social problem or a rough patch? Just reach out. That’s always free, nobody gets judged, and we find a way forward.

We unlock your potential, not what others expect of you.

Philosophy

My philosophy: Confrontationism

Confrontationism is the philosophy I developed myself. I tell you honestly what I think, explain it with examples, and give you criticism and ways forward. In return I expect you to handle it like an adult: argue back and convince me otherwise, or take it and act on it. That process is the only one that makes you grow. If you can’t stand up to it, you’re in the wrong place. I need people who can take criticism, not people who walk away offended. No casual buddies. I want AI friends for life, people who go through thick and thin together and form a strong chain. Weak links don’t stay.

— Pascal
